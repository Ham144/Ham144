# Recruiter Optimization Guide: Dubai & Germany (EU)

This guide provides targeted recommendations to position your profile for international engineering roles in Dubai (UAE) and Germany (EU). 

---

## 1. Frame Yourself as a "Software Engineer" (Drop the "Junior" Label)

**Why:** 
Looking at your codebase (visual node graphs, offline sync pipelines, TCP socket printer buffers, and NTLM SOAP ERP integrations), you are performing mid-level engineering work. Listing "Junior" on your CV will get your application automatically filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) or lead to low-ball salary offers.

*   **Change in CV Header:** Change `Junior Full-Stack Developer` to **`Full-Stack Software Engineer`** or **`Software Engineer (Web & Mobile)`**.
*   **Change in Bio:** Refocus your bio away from "junior holding back interest" and more towards "delivering high-efficiency business tools."
    *   *Draft Bio:* "I am a Full-Stack Software Engineer specialized in React, React Native, and Node.js. I focus on building robust corporate utility tools, including offline-first mobile POS applications, visual approval workflow engines, and automated ERP integrations."

In your current CV, listing basic tools like *Word, Excel, Chrome, PowerPoint, and VS Code* makes your profile look like a beginner's. Recruiters assume you know how to use Chrome and VS Code. Replace them with high-value technical keywords that matching engines scan for.
